What we waterproof

One crew, one system, one guarantee, whether it’s a family home or a commercial block.

Roofs and terraces

Seamless liquid membranes and heat-reflective coatings that stop seepage and cool the rooms below.

Basements and foundations

Crystalline barriers and drainage layers that hold back groundwater and damp walls.

Bathrooms and balconies

Sealed wet areas under the tile, tested with a 48-hour flood check before we finish.

Water tanks and pools

Food-grade, non-toxic linings for drinking water tanks, sumps and swimming pools.

Walls and facades

Breathable exterior coatings that block rain and let trapped moisture escape.

Leak repair

We trace the source first, then repair. No guesswork and no repainting over damp.

Five layers between your building and the rain

Each layer has one job. Applied in order, they keep water out for years.

  1. Surface repairCracks cut open, filled and levelled.
  2. PrimerBonds the membrane to the substrate.
  3. Membrane, first coatFlexible polymer that moves with the structure.
  4. Reinforcement mesh and second coatStrengthens joints and corners.
  5. Protective top coatUV and foot-traffic shield, then a flood test.
